# Product research — 2026-11-14

This is a workflow study, not evidence of this project's capabilities. No competitor branding, interface, testimonials or performance claims are reproduced.

| Source | Observed product emphasis | Design decision here |
|---|---|---|
| [Pricefy](https://www.pricefy.io/) | Price monitoring, catalog matching, rule-based repricing, product-count and frequency tiers | Separate observations, reviewed matches, calculations and execution evidence |
| [Prisync](https://prisync.com/) | Multi-channel tracking and dynamic pricing with a trial onboarding path | Start with catalog import and dry runs; show subscription limits clearly |
| [PriceMole](https://pricemole.io/) | Price/stock monitoring and configurable pricing strategies | Inventory and financial bounds are part of the engine inputs |
| [Price2Spy](https://www.price2spy.com/) | Matching, product variations, reports, repricing, team access | Preserve variation identity and isolate permissions |
| [Priceva](https://priceva.com/) | Price history, competitor comparisons, reporting and rules | Distinguish collected data, computed estimates and actual completed changes |
| [Axon](https://axonmarketing.ir/) | WooCommerce-oriented tools and usage accounting for successful operations | Prioritize WooCommerce; do not count retries as new successful operations |
| [Lidtak](https://lidtak.com/) | Torob monitoring, alerts and WooCommerce price floors | Make minimum prices and integration access visible to operators |

The Raghibot home page could not be retrieved in this environment. Its undocumented APIs or pricing were not inferred. Iranian market read APIs remain unavailable unless a documented, authorized integration is implemented.

Research prices are deliberately not used as this platform's live commercial prices. Initial subscription configuration is development policy and is editable.
